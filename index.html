HOW TO PLAY

  • Batting: Tap numbers 1 to 6 to score runs. If the computer bowls the same number, you are OUT. Otherwise, you score those runs!
  • Bowling: Choose a number 1 to 6. If you match the computer batsman's choice, they are OUT.
  • Controls: Right-click and drag to rotate the 3D stadium, scroll to zoom, and watch the ball flight & hand gestures in real-time!
